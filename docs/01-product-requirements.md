# Product Requirements

## Product Principle

Keep revenue data trustworthy by pairing transparent agent recommendations with human approval and auditable evidence.

## Initial Requirements

- Provide a dashboard for CRM hygiene status and deal review queues.
- Provide deal-level review pages that explain findings and cite evidence.
- Provide approval cards for human review before CRM writes.
- Preserve audit events for agent recommendations, approvals, and rejected changes.
- Support test fixtures, golden outputs, and evals as agent behavior matures.
